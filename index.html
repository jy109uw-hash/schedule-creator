How it works
  • Flexible time = paid working time minus fixed commitments and breaks. Appointment and task-mix percentages are shares of this.
  • Order of placement: fixed commitments → meals → breaks → appointments (spread evenly across days) → task mix → flex.
  • Maximize patient time books every appointment that fits inside your windows, with no daily cap. Task-mix percentages then apply to whatever time is left.
  • Appointments get a notes buffer straight after, counted as client time.
  • Settings save automatically in this browser only — nothing is sent anywhere. Use Export to back them up or move them to another computer.
  • Please don't enter client names or health information; slots are generic.
